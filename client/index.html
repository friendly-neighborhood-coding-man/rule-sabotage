Build the battlefield. Survive your own inventions.

  1. Goal: get all 3 of your pieces from column A to column G. The AI races its 3 pieces the other way. Turns alternate; a coin flip decides who starts.
  2. Actions: each turn you get one move action per piece still on the board. An action moves one piece, or a whole stack of your pieces, one square. Landing on your own piece stacks; landing on an enemy piece captures it and sends it back to its start.
  3. Effects: before your first move you may write one effect in plain words (English or 한국어) and put it on up to 3 cells anywhere that has no piece and no enemy effect. It stays until something enters the cell, then that cell is used up.
  4. Fair for everyone: an effect does exactly the same thing to whoever enters, including you. Directions are absolute (north, south, east, west) or relative to how the piece entered (back, forward).
  5. Your own effects can be removed for free (click them in “Your effects”) or replaced by placing on top of them.
  6. Every new effect, yours or the AI’s, is interpreted, simulated in a sandbox and sent to the Kane referee before it goes live.
