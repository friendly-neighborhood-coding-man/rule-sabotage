  1. Goal: get all 3 of your pieces from column A to column M (13 columns, 9 rows). The AI races its 3 pieces the other way. Turns alternate.
  2. Actions: each turn you get one move action per piece still on the board. An action moves one piece, or a whole stack of your pieces, one square. Landing on your own piece stacks; landing on an enemy piece captures it and sends it back to its start.
  3. Effects: before your first move you may write one effect in plain words (English or 한국어) and put it on up to 3 cells that have no piece and no enemy effect.
  4. No new effects next to pieces (pick the ruleset at the top): V6.1 (A2): you cannot place a new effect on any of the 8 squares around ANY piece, yours or your opponent's (sideways, up/down or diagonal). V6 (A1): only the 8 squares around opponent pieces are closed; squares next to your own pieces are fine. Closed squares are hatched while you pick cells. If one of your chosen cells breaks the rule, the whole placement is refused and nothing is used up, so you can pick again. Effects already on the board stay when a piece walks up to them and still fire, and you can always remove your own effects. The AI plays under exactly the same rule, checked by the same server code.
  5. Fair for everyone: an effect does exactly the same thing to whoever enters, including you. Directions are absolute (north, south, east, west) or relative to how the piece entered (back, forward).
  6. Referee: your text is read by the built-in offline parser (no AI language model), and every new effect, yours or the opponent's, is checked by the engine only. Effects the engine cannot verify on its own are not placed, for both sides. The game ends after 60 turns (30 each).
