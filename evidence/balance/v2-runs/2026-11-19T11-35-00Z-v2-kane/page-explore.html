Read-only data from 24 recorded AI-vs-AI games (run 2026-11-18T14-21-47Z-kane, file sha256 6d1f600e5ad61b43…). Both players are the same AI planner. Nothing on this page states a conclusion.

How the game works

7×7 board. Human pieces start in column A and score in column G; AI pieces start in column G and score in column A. Each turn a player may place ONE effect on up to 3 empty cells, then moves. An effect fires on whichever piece enters the cell, then that cell is used up. Whether an effect is good or bad is judged for the owner of the piece that steps on it, not for the player who installed it. Teleport sends the piece to a random empty square in columns B–F. A shove east moves a piece toward column G; a shove west toward column A.
