What you are asked

  1. Is the observed first-mover / second-mover imbalance reproducible, or could it be chance at this sample size?
  2. Does the initial piece count meaningfully affect fairness?
  3. Are conveyor-belt strategies (stepping on your own trap to be carried forward) legitimate play, or do they cause a measurable gameplay problem?
  4. Can an initial-rule modification improve balance without reducing strategic quality?
  5. Which competing explanation best fits the observations (the planner, the starting rules, or their interaction)?
  6. Which experiment would distinguish those explanations?
  7. What evidence might disprove your preferred explanation?

You may conclude that no change is justified. Base every claim on specific games (use the game ids, e.g. C3-62004-ai). Record each explanation in the notebook: one submission per competing hypothesis, then one final conclusion.
