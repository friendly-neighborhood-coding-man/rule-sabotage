Quick entry (recommended: one text box per entry)

Fill in the lines after each colon and submit. One submission per competing hypothesis (up to 4), then one with entry: conclusion and a verdict. Your text is stored verbatim and never executed.

  • cause: planner · starting-rules · interaction · metric · no-defect · other

Metrics: first_mover_win_rate (First-mover win rate (decisive games)); abs_first_mover_edge (Distance of first-mover win rate from 50%); human_side_win_rate (Human-side win rate); more_pieces_side_win_rate (Win rate of the side that started with more pieces); mean_turns (Mean game length (turns)); turn_limit_rate (Share of games reaching the turn limit); conveyor_per_game (Conveyor moves per game (own trap carries the piece forward)); self_trigger_share (Share of triggers on own traps); lead_changes (Lead changes per game); comeback_rate (Share of decisive games won by a side that trailed); effect_entropy (Effect diversity (normalized entropy)); unused_cell_rate (Share of trap cells never triggered); turn1_score_rate (Share of games in which the first mover brings a piece across on turn 1); treated_win_rate (Head-to-head strength of a changed planner against production)
