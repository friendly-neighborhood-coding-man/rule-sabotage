Background: earlier V2 findings (NOT part of this dataset)

This page summarises a different, earlier experiment on 3-versus-3 games with other seeds. Do not mix its numbers with the 100-game dataset.
  • V2 (draft PR #4) investigated 3-versus-3 games only, on seeds 1000–1011 (exploration), 3200–3215 (development) and 7100–7115 (holdout).
  • Kane proposed one hypothesis of its own (HK1: the V0.3 prior undervalues Teleport). Tested by setting the Teleport weight to its measured value: INCONCLUSIVE.
  • H3 (the planner's trap-cell search counts benefit to its own pieces, so it builds traps its own pieces then use) was SUPPORTED twice on independent seeds.
  • The H3 correction K1 (remove own-benefit from the cell search completely) reduced self-triggering but was a REGRESSION: mean game length rose from about 14.5 to 27.1 turns and the changed planner lost head-to-head.
  • Changing Teleport weights did not produce a convincing improvement. Production settings were not changed.
  • Kane's ability to design experiments independently was not demonstrated.
