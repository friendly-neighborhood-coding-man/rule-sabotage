Submit a hypothesis

A hypothesis is a rule you think the game follows, stated in your own words. One form per hypothesis. You do not have to give a hypothesis for every aspect of the game, and you may submit competing hypotheses for the same behaviour. If you cannot decide, abstain instead: abstaining is a valid answer. Everything you submit is stored exactly as written and is never overwritten; submit a new form to revise.
